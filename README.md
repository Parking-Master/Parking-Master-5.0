## Welcome
What's new with Parking Master 5.0?
- New tropical main menu design, since Parking Master 5.0 is based in Florida
- New Highly realistic graphics with changing environments on every level
- New Performance Slider in settings
- New Physics Engine using Cannon.js so the game has realistic physics now
- New simple login/signup pages
- New leaderboard showing the best players
- Brought back the license feature from [Parking Master 3.0](https://github.com/Parking-Master/Parking-Master-3.0)
- 15 Brand new fun levels
- 6 New handcrafted cars
- New multiplayer experience
- Improved frontend/backend security

## How do I play it?

### Controls
Keyboard Controls:
> <kbd>↑</kbd> Gas Pedal<br>
> <kbd>←</kbd> Steer left<br>
> <kbd>→</kbd> Steer right<br>
> <kbd>Enter</kbd> Park<br>
> <kbd>P</kbd> Shift into park (shortcut)<br>
> <kbd>R</kbd> Shift into reverse (shortcut)<br>
> <kbd>D</kbd> Shift into drive (shortcut)<br>
> <kbd>B</kbd> Brake Pedal<br>
> <kbd>[</kbd> Toggle left blinker<br>
> <kbd>]</kbd> Toggle right blinker<br>
> <kbd>V</kbd> Toggle view<br>

Gamepad Controls:
> <kbd>RT</kbd> Gas Pedal<br>
> <kbd>←L</kbd> Steer left<br>
> <kbd>L→</kbd> Steer right<br>
> <kbd>A</kbd> Park<br>
> <kbd>DPad↑</kbd> Shift up<br>
> <kbd>DPad↓</kbd> Shift down<br>
> <kbd>LT</kbd> Brake Pedal<br>
> <kbd>←DPad</kbd> Toggle left blinker<br>
> <kbd>DPad→</kbd> Toggle right blinker<br>
> <kbd>RB</kbd> Toggle view<br>
